# 3. Sources by licence first

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-11-15.

## Context

The packs draw on standards, vendor frameworks and books. The repository is Apache-2.0 and must stay under one licence.

## Decision

Public domain and CC BY sources may be adapted with attribution. Books and talks contribute principles only, in our own words, with a citation. Proprietary, non-commercial and share-alike sources are cited by name and never copied. Security rules are built on NIST rather than OWASP to avoid a share-alike pack. PyMuPDF (AGPL) is not used; pypdf (BSD) is.

## Consequences

Every rule carries a source block; ATTRIBUTIONS.md and NOTICE are generated from them. Two vendor licences are confirmed at ingestion rather than assumed.
